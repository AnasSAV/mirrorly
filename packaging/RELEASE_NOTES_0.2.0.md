**Mirrorly 0.2** adds drag and drop: move photos and files between your iPhone and your PC.

### New: drag and drop files

- **iPhone → Windows.** Copy photos or files on the iPhone (Photos: *Share › Copy Photo*; Files: *touch and hold › Copy*). A chip appears on Mirrorly's bar: drag it onto the desktop, into Explorer, or into any app. Full quality, and files keep their names.
- **Windows → iPhone.** Drop files on the mirrored screen. Photos and videos are pasted into the app you have open (a chat, a note, an email). Documents (PDF, Word, …) are saved with their names in a **Mirrorly** folder in the Files app, and put on the clipboard too.
- Clear messages on screen while files are copied, pasted or saved.

### Also

- A redesigned [README](https://github.com/AnasSAV/mirrorly#readme) with a full guide, diagrams and measured performance.

### Notes

- iOS doesn't let other programs write to the top level of *On My iPhone*, so documents go into a Mirrorly folder inside the Files folder of an app that shares files (VLC or Chrome, for example). Tip: mark that folder as a Favorite in Files.
- Up to 200 MB per drop. Copied text on the iPhone is never read.
