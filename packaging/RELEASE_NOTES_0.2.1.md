**Mirrorly 0.2.1**: disconnect any time, switch between iPhones, and a smoother bar.

### New

- **Disconnect and connect.** A power button on the bar disconnects. Mirrorly lets go of the iPhone (the camera and microphone are free again) and stays disconnected, even over USB or Wi-Fi, until you connect again.
- **Choose your iPhone.** The disconnected screen lists every iPhone Mirrorly knows, marked *Plugged in* or *Wi-Fi, when nearby*. Click one to mirror it, or **Forget** it. If two iPhones are plugged in at once, Mirrorly asks which one to use.

### Improved

- **The bar is smoother.** It fades and slides in instead of flickering when you point at the window, and stays attached when you drag the window or snap it with Win + arrow keys.
- **Only one Mirrorly at a time.** Opening Mirrorly again brings the running window forward. Two copies could both hold the iPhone, so the camera stayed busy after disconnecting one.
