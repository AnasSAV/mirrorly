**Mirrorly 0.3**: one clipboard for your PC and your iPhone.

### New: shared clipboard

- **Windows → iPhone.** Text and pictures you copy on the PC are on the iPhone's clipboard right away: press **Ctrl+V** in Mirrorly, or touch and hold › **Paste** on the iPhone.
- **Files from Explorer.** Copy files in Explorer, then press **Ctrl+V** in Mirrorly: photos are pasted into the open app, documents are saved by name in the Mirrorly folder in Files.
- **iPhone → Windows.** Text copied on the iPhone pastes anywhere on the PC. Photos and files copied on the iPhone paste into Explorer, chats and email, and a photo pastes as a picture into Paint, Word and the like, the right way up.
- **The newest copy wins**, on either side.

### Privacy

- Anything a password manager marks as private on the PC is never sent to the iPhone.
- Text from the iPhone is kept out of Windows' clipboard history and cloud clipboard.

### Notes

- On the iPhone, the paste suggestion above the keyboard says *"Paste from dtpasteboardd"*: that is the iOS service Mirrorly hands the copy to, and iOS doesn't let it be renamed.
- A document pasted into a chat app may get a generic name; to keep the name, send it from Files › Mirrorly with the Share button.
