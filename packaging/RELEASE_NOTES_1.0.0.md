**Mirrorly 1.0**: a new logo and a new look, in light and dark, on everything that came before.

Mirrorly shows your iPhone or iPad in a window on your Windows PC and lets you use it with your mouse and keyboard, over USB or Wi-Fi, adding about 1 ms of delay on the PC. No Mac, no jailbreak, no account, and nothing leaves your own devices.

### New in 1.0: Titanium & Ice

- **A new logo**, on the app icon, the installer, the app's own pages and the iPad's Home Screen icon.
- **Light and dark.** Mirrorly follows Windows' app mode and changes as soon as Windows does. The iPad's page follows the iPad's own setting.
- **New colours everywhere**: the window, the bar, the installer, the README and the wiki.

### Everything in Mirrorly

- **Mirror and control:** click to tap, drag to swipe, scroll with the wheel, and type on your keyboard.
- **USB and Wi-Fi:** connect by cable once, then unplug. It moves between the two by itself.
- **Drag and drop, one clipboard:** move photos and files both ways, and copy on one side to paste on the other.
- **Screenshots and recordings:** F6 and F7, at the phone's full quality.
- **iPad as a second monitor:** one click, at the iPad's resolution and 60 fps, used with the PC's mouse and keyboard.

### Requirements

Windows 10 (1809+) or 11 x64, [Apple Devices](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9np83lwlpz9k), and an **iPhone or iPad on iOS / iPadOS 27 or later** with Developer Mode on. For the iPad display: the iPad and PC on the same Wi-Fi (network profile *Private*), and administrator rights once. Recordings are HEVC: Windows plays them with the HEVC Video Extensions.
